Editorial Edition
CHAPTER 01
Personalize Your Ledger

Open Profile & Settings from the top bar to upload an avatar and update your university contact details.

CHAPTER 02
Capture Daily Entries

Use the Quick Ledger Entry block on Overview to log campus meals, transit, or allowance deposits in seconds.

CHAPTER 03
Define Category Limits

In Budgets, set monthly caps per category. Automated alerts notify you at 50%, 80%, and 100% usage.

CHAPTER 04
Settle Peer Balances

Track shared lunches or project fees under Loans, and mark them settled as soon as they're squared up.

CHAPTER 05
Watch Your Financial Health Score

Your Financial Insights card computes a live savings health ratio from monthly income and spending. Keeping it above 70% builds a resilient semester cushion.
