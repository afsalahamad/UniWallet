System Onboarding Guide

Mastery v2.4
STEP 01
Secure Your Identity

Navigate to the Identity Settings (bottom of sidebar). Upload a profile photo and verify your name. This personalizes your entire dashboard and secures your local data vault.

STEP 02
Log Daily Transactions

Use the Quick Log on the dashboard. Switch between Expense and Income tabs. Select a category, enter the amount, and save. The Balance Card updates instantly.

STEP 03
Set Spending Boundaries

In the Budgeting portal, define limits for categories like 'Food' or 'Entertainment'. The system monitors these in real-time—bars turn Red if you exceed 90%.

STEP 04
Manage Commitments

Add monthly bills to Recurring. Use the Pay Now button to officially post them as expenses. Use the Loans portal to track who you owe or who owes you—look out for Overdue badges.

STEP 05
Monitor Your Financial Health

The Insights Panel on the dashboard calculates a dynamic health score. A score above 70% indicates excellent financial discipline. Use the History portal to audit your full audit trail and search for specific past spends.
